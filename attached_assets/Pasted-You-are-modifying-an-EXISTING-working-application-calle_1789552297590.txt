==================================================

3. Record the delay duration.

6. Record the date/time of the change.
7. Update all affected bookings.
8. Update affected farmer dashboards.
9. Update farmer booking details.
10. Create a notification for affected farmers.
11. Clearly display "Delayed" status.

"Your procurement slot has been delayed.

11:00 AM

Reason:
High queue volume."

Do not simply change the visible time without recording the change.
